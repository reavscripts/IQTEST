Quick interpretation

An IQ score of 105 is an estimate of performance on reasoning tasks such as pattern recognition, logic, and problem solving. Exact labels vary by test, but the score helps you understand where you likely fall relative to the general population.

How it compares to average

Many IQ scales are centered around an average near 100. A score higher than 100 is typically above average; lower than 100 is typically below average. Your exact standing depends on the test’s standardization and the conditions under which you took it.

What could influence this score

  • Focus & sleep: fatigue and distractions can reduce accuracy.
  • Time pressure: speeded tests can lower scores for careful thinkers.
  • Format familiarity: prior exposure to puzzle styles may help.

How to use the result

Treat the score as a snapshot. If you want a more stable estimate, retake a similar test after a week in the same conditions and compare the results. The most useful insight is the pattern: are you consistently average, above average, or fluctuating?
