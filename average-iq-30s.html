People often search for the “average IQ in your 30s”, but it’s important to know that IQ tests use age-based norms. That means scores are compared to others in a similar age group.

Key point

Because scores are normalized by age, an “average” score is typically close to 100 across many age groups—what changes is the type and difficulty of tasks used to measure reasoning at each stage.

Why online results can differ

  • Short tests have more measurement noise.
  • Devices and time pressure affect performance.
  • Motivation and fatigue matter.
