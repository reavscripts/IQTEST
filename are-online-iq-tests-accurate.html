Online IQ tests can provide a useful estimate of reasoning performance, but accuracy depends on the quality of the test, the scoring method, and your testing conditions.

What affects accuracy most

  • Standardization and question design
  • Time pressure and device differences
  • Distractions, fatigue, and motivation

How to get more reliable results

Take the test in a quiet setting, avoid multitasking, and compare results across multiple sessions. Consistency is a stronger signal than a single score.
