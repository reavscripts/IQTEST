Short answer

An IQ score of 145 can be considered “good” depending on the benchmark you use. Many scales place the average around 100, so scores above that are generally above average. Scores below that can still be normal—especially when factors like stress, time pressure, or distraction are present.

What “good” really means

IQ is not the same as success, creativity, or wisdom. It mainly reflects performance on standardized reasoning tasks. A “good” score is one that is interpreted correctly: as an estimate, with awareness of limitations.

How to think about your result

  • Look at the score range, not just the number.
  • Consider your conditions while taking the test.
  • Use it as a baseline to track progress.
