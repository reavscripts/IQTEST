People often ask “what percentile is an IQ of 100?” Percentiles translate scores into comparisons: a percentile tells you roughly how many people you scored higher than.

Why you’ll see different answers online

  • Different IQ tests use different norms and standard deviations.
  • Short online tests have more measurement error.
  • Scores can shift based on sleep, focus, and time pressure.

Best way to interpret your percentile

Use your percentile as an approximate indicator. If you want more stability, compare multiple results taken under similar conditions and focus on the overall range.
