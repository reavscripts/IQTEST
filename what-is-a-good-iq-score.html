IQ ranges at a glance

Many tests are standardized so the average is near 100. Scores around 90–109 are often described as the average range. Above that is above average; lower can be below average, depending on the scale.

Percentiles explain “good” better than numbers

A percentile tells you how you compare to others. Two tests can both report “120” but represent different percentiles depending on how the test is designed.

Context matters

  • Age norms and population samples vary.
  • Speed vs accuracy changes outcomes.
  • Practice effect can inflate scores on similar puzzle types.
